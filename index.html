            Domande · FAQ

            Is my ticket good for any day?

            Yes. A general admission ticket is good for one entry on any day, any time during public hours.

            Can I bring a bag?

            Small bags, cinch bags and purses up to 9″ × 10″ × 12″ are fine. Backpacks aren’t allowed, but exceptions are made for parents with infants and guests with medical needs.

            Can I bring my own food or water bottle?

            No outside food or drinks, except what you need for infants or medical and dietary needs. Reusable bottles, tumblers and cans aren’t allowed either.

            Where do I go for Festa Mass?

            Enter through the South Gate, which opens at 9:30am Sunday. Mass begins at 10:30am, followed by the procession and Benediction. Please bring three non-perishable food items for the Hunger Task Force food drive.

            Are strollers and umbrellas allowed?

            Yes on the grounds. They aren’t allowed inside the American Family Insurance Amphitheater.

            Who runs Festa?

            Festa Italiana is produced by the Italian Community Center of Milwaukee and run entirely by volunteers. It’s the ICC’s largest fundraiser, supporting Italian culture and community programs all year long.
